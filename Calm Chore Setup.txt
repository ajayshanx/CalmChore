Privacy & Data Handling:
Now that children from different families can connect as friends, this app handles children's personal data beyond a single household, not just a private family tool - this baseline applies regardless of how many families end up using it:
- Every child profile is created by a parent, not self-registered by the child. This is the app's structural stand-in for parental consent, paired with the explicit Terms & Conditions and Parental Consent Notice (see "Calm Chore Terms and Conditions.docx") shown and accepted (checkbox + confirmation email) during parent Create Account - see "Create Account" above.
- Data collected per actor: parents (First Name, Last Name, Email); children (Username, Nickname, accent colour, chore activity, points/streak history, and a proof photo submitted per chore - see deletion rule below). Notably, a child's real/legal name is never collected at all - there is no such field anywhere in the app. Username is a parent-chosen login handle (never shown to other families), and Nickname is the only identifier shown anywhere in the app, including to friends.
- Proof photo retention: a proof photo is only ever needed to support the parent's one-time validation decision. It is automatically and permanently deleted the moment the parent sets the chore instance's status (Complete, Partially Complete, or Incomplete) - it is never retained afterward, even within the child's own family. Only the outcome (status, awarded points, non-completion reason) is kept. See "Calm Chore Creation.txt" for the instance-level rule.
- What's visible to an approved friend (a different family): Nickname, Chore Streak, Total Completed Chores, Total Points, Tier, and the last 5 completed chore names with date and points. Nothing else - proof photos are never visible to friends even during the brief window before deletion.
- What's never shared outside a child's own family, under any circumstance: proof photos (which are short-lived, see above), passcodes, email addresses, notification settings, and full chore/redemption history beyond the last-5 summary above.
- Friend search works by Nickname only - there's no real-name field to search by in the first place, and Username (the login handle) is never searchable or shown to other families - so a child can't be located by someone who isn't already an intended contact (see "My Friends" tab in "Child Login Options.txt").
- A parent can request deletion of a child's profile and its associated data (photos, points history, chore history). Deletion should also remove that child from any friends' connections on the other side, not leave a dangling reference.
- Because this may extend to children outside the immediate family, treat it as in scope for children's privacy regulations (e.g. COPPA in the US, and equivalent rules elsewhere) once used beyond a single household. A first-draft, COPPA-oriented Terms & Conditions and Parental Consent Notice has been written ("Calm Chore Terms and Conditions.docx", shown/accepted per "Create Account" above) covering the consent gate, data collected, proof-photo deletion, Friends disclosure, parental rights, and retention - but it is explicitly marked as needing licensed-attorney review before real users see it, and is not a substitute for that review.

Timezone:
Streaks, Chore Freezes, the Weekly Streak Bonus, and daily/weekly boundaries generally are all defined in terms of "a day" or "a calendar week" - these need a single, unambiguous timezone per family to mean anything (e.g. is 11pm on the East Coast still "today" for a family that just moved to the West Coast, or for grandparents managing a grandchild's account from another country?). Use the timezone captured from the device at parent Create Account as that family's timezone of record for all children under the account, editable later from Setup --> Profiles if a family relocates. All "day" and "week" boundaries (freeze auto-apply, streak counting, Weekly Streak Bonus calculation) are evaluated in that stored timezone, not the server's or an individual device's current timezone, so behavior stays consistent even if a child logs in from a different timezone while traveling.

Add to Home Screen Prompt:
The first time the app is opened in a browser on a given device, by any actor (a parent, an additional parent, or a child logging in directly - not applicable to a Parent-Managed child, who has no login of their own), and the app is not already installed to that device's home screen, show a prompt encouraging the user to add the app icon to their home screen.
- On Android / Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet), the prompt includes a real "Add to Home Screen" button that triggers the browser's own install flow directly - effectively a one-tap action (plus the browser's own confirmation step, which cannot be skipped).
- On iOS Safari, which does not allow an app to trigger installation directly, the prompt instead shows a short visual walkthrough pointing to the Share button and then "Add to Home Screen", since only the user can complete those steps.
- On desktop browsers, the equivalent install prompt is shown where supported (e.g. Chrome, Edge); this is a secondary case for the app.
The prompt should be dismissible ("Not now") without blocking use of the app. It should not reappear once the app is installed, but can reappear on a later login if the app still isn't installed on that device, rather than being a permanent one-time-only miss.
This is in addition to, not instead of, the step-by-step "how to add the app icon to your home screen" instructions already covered in the About section for each login type.


Create Account:
Create account should allow entry of First Name, Last Name and Email.
Before the account can be created, the parent must be shown the Terms & Conditions and Parental Consent Notice (see "Calm Chore Terms and Conditions.docx" in the same folder) and check a box confirming they are the parent/legal guardian, have read the notice, and consent to the data practices it describes. The "Create Account" button stays disabled until this box is checked - this single in-app acceptance, tied to the parent's own account, is the consent record; no separate confirmation email is sent. The notice's version number and the date/time of this first acceptance are stored against the parent's account (this "earliest accepted" timestamp is what's shown later in Setup --> Privacy Policy, and doesn't change on later re-reads). If the notice is later updated in a way that changes what's collected, used, or shared (a material change), the parent must re-accept the current version before the account can continue being used, which additionally records that version's acceptance date - a minor wording clarification does not require re-acceptance.
This should also have an option to "Add Profiles"
Profiles could be for additional parents or for children.

Additional Parent profile:
The additional parent profile addition should allow the addition of further parents who can login and administer the same set of children.
Each parent added should have the following information captured:
- First Name, Last Name, Email.
Adding a parent sends an invite email with a link to set their own password (they are never given or told the first parent's password, and the first parent doesn't set one on their behalf). On first login, before reaching anything else, the invited parent is shown the same Terms & Conditions and Parental Consent Notice as the original Create Account flow and must accept it the same way (checkbox) - each parent with access to a child's data needs to have consented individually, not just the parent who happened to create the account. Until the invite is accepted, that parent shows as "Invited" (not yet active) in Setup --> Profiles.

The children profile definition should allow the following information:
- Parent-Managed (Y/N) - for a child too young to have their own device / login. When set to Yes, a Username and Passcode are not required for that child, and the parent instead tracks that child's chores from their own login (see "Manage for [Child]" tab in "Parent Login Options.txt"). Can be switched off later once the child is ready for their own login - existing points, streak and tier history carry over unchanged.
- UserName (manually entered by the parent, app-wide duplicate validation to be done) - not required if Parent-Managed is Yes. This is a login handle only, chosen freely by the parent - the app has no separate real-name field, so nothing requires this to be the child's actual/legal name (a parent is free to use one if they want, but it isn't asked for or needed).
- Passcode (6 digit numeric) - not required if Parent-Managed is Yes
- Nickname (to be filled in by the child - to be done on first login and can be modified at any time). For a Parent-Managed child, the parent fills this in instead, and can update it later. The nickname is the only identifier shown anywhere in the app once set - on the calendar, in chore lists, to friends, everywhere. Until a child's first login, parent-facing lists (Setup --> Profiles, Child Progress, Validate Chores) show the Username in its place as a placeholder label.
- Selected colour (must be unique within an account) - picked by the child from a curated palette of vibrant accent colours (distinct from the calm green used on the parent side). Suggested on first login alongside the nickname, and can be updated by the child at any time from their profile. Used to colour the chore calendar and applied app-wide as the child's personal accent theme (e.g. header accents, badges) throughout their own login. For a Parent-Managed child, the parent picks this instead.

On each further login, the parent sees a Setup menu option (through the "Setup" word or a gear or spanner icon).
Under the setup, the parents see Profiles, Notifications, and Privacy Policy.

Setup --> Privacy Policy:
This shows the full Terms & Conditions and Parental Consent Notice (see "Calm Chore Terms and Conditions.docx") in a plain, readable in-app view - not just at Create Account, but permanently accessible any time a parent wants to check it, since this is reasonably something a parent may want to revisit rather than only see once during signup.
- At the top, show the earliest date/time this parent accepted the notice (e.g. "You first accepted this on 25 July 2026"), which stays fixed even if they've since re-accepted a newer version after a material change - and clearly flag if a newer version exists that they haven't accepted yet.
- Ahead of the full text, call out the two things parents are most likely to want reassurance on, in plain language and without needing to read the whole notice: (1) proof photos are automatically and permanently deleted the moment a parent reviews and rates a chore submission - never stored long-term or shown to anyone else; and (2) Calm Chore never asks for or stores a child's real/legal name - only a parent-chosen username (login only) and a child-chosen nickname (the only identifier shown anywhere in the app, including to friends).
- The full notice text follows below that summary, unchanged from what was accepted at Create Account (see "Create Account" above).
This tab is read-only information - re-acceptance, when needed after a material change, happens through the acceptance prompt described under "Create Account", not from this Setup screen.
